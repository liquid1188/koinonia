Welcome to Koinonia

A curated Catholic commons — connecting the faithful with the institutes, publishers, media, sacred art, and intellectual life of the Church.

Community Directory Formation Fellowship
Explore the Directory Join the Community
“The Eucharist is the sacrament of the unity of the Church, which results from the fact that many are one in Christ.” — St. Thomas Aquinas

Koinonia (κοινωνία) — fellowship, joint participation, communion. The word appears throughout the New Testament to describe the shared life of believers, rooted in the Eucharist and ordered toward the unity Christ willed for His Church.

This portal gathers the richness of Catholic intellectual, liturgical, and cultural life into a single commons: a place where scholars and seekers, parishes and publishers, artists and apostolates can find one another and build the kind of communion the digital world so often fragments.

Koinonia is an initiative of the Likoudis Legacy Foundation, a 501(c)(3) research institute dedicated to ecumenism, interreligious dialogue, and religious liberty.

“They devoted themselves to the apostles’ teaching and to fellowship, to the breaking of bread and to prayer.” Acts 2:42
