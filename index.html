About Koinonia

Koinonia — from the Greek κοινωνία, meaning fellowship, communion, and shared participation — names the bond that unites Christians in the Body of Christ. It is, in the fullest sense, the life of the Church herself.

This portal gathers the richness of the Catholic digital world into a single, curated commons. It is a project of the Likoudis Legacy Foundation, a 501(c)(3) research institute dedicated to ecumenism, interreligious dialogue, and religious liberty.

Whether you are a scholar seeking resources, a catechist looking for formation, or a young Catholic seeking fellowship in a fragmented digital landscape — Koinonia is for you.

In Illo Uno Unum.
In the One, we are one.
