About Koinonia

Koinonia — from the Greek κοινωνία, meaning fellowship, communion, and shared participation — names the bond that unites Christians in the Body of Christ.

This portal gathers the richness of the Catholic digital world into a single, curated commons: 120+ institutes and universities, publishers, media outlets, podcasts, sacred art, prayer, forums, and service organizations.

A project of the Likoudis Legacy Foundation, a 501(c)(3) research institute dedicated to ecumenism, interreligious dialogue, and religious liberty.
